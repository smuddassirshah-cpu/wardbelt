# Decisions

Every choice made where `docs/PLAN.md` was silent, or where an implementer deviated from it,
in the order the build made them. Each entry carries the same six fields: date, decision,
reason, alternatives considered, whether it deviates from PLAN.md (y/n), and the stage that
made it (the stage is the section heading; the id repeats it). The source of every entry from
stage 0 to stage 7 is the Decisions log in `STATE.md`, written by the orchestrator from each
subagent's report; the wording here is tightened, never changed in substance. "None recorded"
means the log names no rejected alternative. The section 9 trade-offs from PLAN.md are listed
last because they were decided before stage 0.
